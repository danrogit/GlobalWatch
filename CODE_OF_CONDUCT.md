# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

GlobalWatch welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives.

Please keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software and its documentation.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Giving and receiving technical feedback with care.
- Assuming good intent while still being precise.
- Respecting that geopolitical data can affect real people and communities.
- Making space for newcomers to ask practical questions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Deliberately spreading unverified claims as fact.
- Publishing secrets, tokens, or private credentials.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's listed contact method.
