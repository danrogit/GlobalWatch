🟢 Core principle (this guides everything)

If it affects how states act toward each other, toward their population, or toward power structures — it belongs.

If not → ignore it.

✅ PRIMARY CATEGORIES (you should focus on these)
1️⃣ Military & security events

(Obvious, but tightly scoped)

Include:

Armed clashes

Airstrikes

Missile launches

Military operations

Troop deployments with action

Terror attacks

Cross-border incidents

Military escalation / de-escalation

Exclude:

Military parades

Exercises unless tied to escalation

Veteran stories

Historic retrospectives

2️⃣ Diplomacy & international relations

(High value, often ignored by other platforms)

Include:

Diplomatic expulsions

Recognition / derecognition of states

Peace talks

Mediation efforts

Treaty signings

Summits (NATO, EU, G7, SCO, etc.)

Severed or restored diplomatic relations

Exclude:

Soft culture exchanges

Opinion pieces

3️⃣ Sanctions, trade & economic warfare

(This is modern warfare)

Include:

Sanctions imposed / lifted

Asset freezes

Trade bans

Export controls

Energy embargoes

Secondary sanctions

Currency restrictions

Exclude:

Stock market noise

Corporate earnings

4️⃣ Elections, coups & regime change

(Very important — but rules matter)

Include:

National elections

Disputed election results

Military coups

States of emergency

Power transfers

Constitutional crises

Mass election-related violence

Exclude:

Local municipal elections (unless violent or symbolic)

Polling speculation

5️⃣ Protests & civil unrest (state-level only)

Include:

Nationwide protests

Violent crackdowns

Martial law

Internet shutdowns

Curfews

Use of military or police at scale

Exclude:

Small local protests

Lifestyle activism

Single-issue marches without state response

6️⃣ Borders, territory & sovereignty

(Core geopolitics)

Include:

Border closures

Territorial claims

Annexation attempts

Buffer zones

Maritime disputes

Airspace violations

Exclude:

Tourism border issues

Routine visa changes

7️⃣ Government actions with geopolitical impact

Include:

Emergency laws

National security legislation

War declarations

Mobilization orders

Defense spending surges

Intelligence disclosures

Exclude:

Routine domestic policy

Cultural legislation

8️⃣ Information warfare & state narratives (careful but valuable)

Include:

State-led disinformation campaigns

Internet blackouts

Media bans

Propaganda escalations

Platform bans (TikTok, X, Telegram, etc.)

Exclude:

Social media drama

Influencer controversies

❌ What GlobeWatch should NOT cover

This is just as important.

Do NOT cover:

Crime (unless terrorism)

Accidents

Celebrity politics

Opinion pieces

Editorials

Human interest stories

Climate stories unless geopolitical (e.g. water conflicts)

Economy unless sanctions / state action

If it doesn’t involve state power, it doesn’t belong.
